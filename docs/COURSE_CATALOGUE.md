# Offset Aware course catalogue

What each training pack contains, who takes each course, and which jobs add which courses.

Every course is a video with switchable English subtitles, a quiz (80% to pass), an acknowledgement the person ticks, and a one-page summary to read or print.

**How people get their courses:** everyone takes the *all staff* courses. The other courses are *by role*: the administrator gives each person one or more jobs, and each job adds its courses. The administrator can also change who must take any course in **Settings → Training modules**.

Lengths are approximate.

## Notes

- Scenarios in the videos are illustrative, not real cases.
- Videos for ordinary staff don't quote section or clause numbers. Videos for specialists (Board, auditors, officers, risk owners) name the rules they need, once.
- The one-page summaries have blank lines for your own contacts, such as where to report fraud. Tell staff what to write there.
