# Employee User Manual: Offset Aware

Welcome! This is where you complete your required security awareness training. It takes a few minutes per module.

## Getting started

Your IT administrator will give you three things: the address of Aware, your **Employee ID**, and a starting password.

### Signing in

1. Open the address your IT department gave you.
2. If your browser says "Your connection is not private", that is expected for a server inside your company. Choose **Advanced**, then continue to the site.
3. Enter your Employee ID and password, and click **Sign In**.

### Your first sign-in

The first time you sign in you will be asked to choose a new password before you can go any further. It must be at least 12 characters, and different from the starting one. Pick something only you would know.

There is no screen for changing your password later. If you forget it, ask your administrator to reset it. They will give you a temporary one, and you will choose your own again at your next sign-in.

## Completing your training

At the top of the page, a ring shows how many of your modules are done, with how many you have left to do, and **Up to date** once you have finished them all. Below it, each module is a tile: the ones still **To do** first, then the ones that are **Done**, with the date each was finished and the date it needs doing again.

Your modules are the ones your administrator has given you: the training that applies to you, plus any for your job (for example IT, branch staff or managers). If nothing has been given to you yet, the page says **Nothing to do yet**, and you may still take any module. Anything else you are welcome to take is under **More modules you are welcome to take**.

### 1. Pick a module and watch the video

Click **Start** on a module's tile. The video opens underneath. Watch it to the end. You cannot skip ahead, but if you close the page you carry on from where you were. Turn on subtitles with the **CC** button if you want them. Under the video there is a **one-page summary** to read or print. The quiz appears when the video finishes.

### 2. Take the quiz

Answer every question, tick the box to confirm you have completed the training and will follow it, then click **Submit answers**. You need **80% or more** to pass. A module is finished when you have watched the video and passed the quiz. If you stop part-way, its tile says what is left, for example **Video watched, quiz to do**, and its button takes you back in.

### 3. If you do not pass

The quiz closes and tells you your score. Click **Retake quiz** for a fresh one, with the questions in a different order. You can retake it as many times as you need.

If you do not pass three times in a row on the same module, you will be asked to watch the video again before trying again. That is the quickest way to the answers.

### 4. Your certificate

Each finished module has a **Certificate** button on its tile. When all your required modules are done, your status turns **Up to date** and a **Download completion certificate (PDF)** button appears at the top of the page. Your administrator can see your progress on their own screen, so you do not need to send it to them unless they ask.
