## What's new in 1.3.1

- **Course packs only:** the installer now offers just the 4 packs (ISO 27001, HIPAA, SAMA for banks, SAMA for finance companies). The 5 old starter courses are removed, because the packs now cover the same topics in newer, fuller courses. Servers that already have them can keep them.
- **Renew early:** staff can redo a course in its last 30 days, when the reminders start. They stay "done" while they renew.
- **Progress shows 100% only when everything is done.**
- **Safer starting passwords:** a **Done** button hides the password, and it also hides when you leave the screen.
- **Fixes:** the guides say 12-character passwords, and quiz times are saved correctly.
