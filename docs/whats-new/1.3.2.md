## What's new in 1.3.2

- **Three new course packs:**
  - **GDPR:** 11 courses, for any organisation handling personal data of people in the EU or UK.
  - **ISO 42001 (AI):** 14 courses, for any organisation that builds, buys or uses AI.
  - **SOC 2:** 7 courses, for service organisations whose customers ask for a SOC 2 report.

  That makes 78 courses in seven packs. Each course has a video with subtitles, a 10-question quiz and a one-page summary. Add them in the installer, or later under **Courses → Course packs**.
- **A new Courses screen:** who takes what, jobs, course packs and how long training stays valid now have their own place in the menu. Settings keeps password resets, email, HTTPS and updates.
- **Shorter Who takes what:** each set of training is one line. Click **Show courses** to open it.
- **Training history for auditors:** the CSV download has a new **Framework** column, so a SOC 2, ISO or HIPAA auditor can filter to their own courses.
- **Clearer badges:** ISO 27001 shows as **27** and ISO 42001 as **42**.
