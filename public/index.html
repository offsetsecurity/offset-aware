The menu on the left has these screens.

Employees see only their own training: they pick a module, watch the video, then take the quiz.

Adding people

One at a time, or a whole list.

One at a time

  1. Open Add people.
  2. Under Add one person, fill in Employee ID, Full Name and Email. The Employee ID must be unique.
  3. Under Training, each set of training is a card. Switch on the ones that apply to the person, then open a card to choose their job. The bar underneath adds up how many courses that comes to.
  4. Click Add person. If email is set up, the person is sent their sign-in details.

A whole list

Under Add a list (CSV), upload a file with three columns, Employee ID, Name and Email, then click Import. Two more columns are optional: Job and Training, using the names listed on that screen, with a semicolon between them when there is more than one. Nobody is added twice: upload the list again with those columns filled in and, for people already here, they replace what the person had.

Who does which training

Training comes in sets: the built-in courses, and each course pack. In Settings, on the Training tab, each set applies to everyone, to chosen people, or to nobody. A set starts as chosen people, so nobody is given it until you say so.

  • One person: switch on their training when you add them, or later with Training beside their name on Employees.
  • Many people: tick them on Employees and click Assign training to selected. Search or filter the list first, and the box at the top ticks everyone shown. It adds to what each person has, or takes it away, and leaves the rest of their list alone.
  • Everybody: set the training to Everyone on the Training tab in Settings.

A job brings its own courses, and the set it belongs to. Employees warns you when people have no training to do.

The first time someone signs in

Every new account gets a starting password of its own: emailed to the person when email is set up, and shown to you once. At first sign-in the person is made to choose their own password before they can go any further. It must be at least 8 characters, and different from the starting one.

Ask people to sign in soon after you add them. Until they do, anyone who knows their Employee ID could sign in with the starting password.

Resetting a password

Open Settings. On the Password reset tab, enter the person's Employee ID and the email on their account, and click Reset password. Both have to match, so the wrong person is never reset. Aware makes a new starting password, emails it to them when email is set up, and shows it to you once. They must choose their own the next time they sign in.

Removing people

Tick the people on the Employees tab and click Delete selected. This cannot be undone.

Training and quizzes

Each person has the modules their training brings: the ones for everyone it applies to, plus those for their job. Anything else on the server is optional for them.

What an employee does

  1. Choose a module.
  2. Watch the video to the end. They cannot skip ahead.
  3. The quiz unlocks. Answer every question and submit.
  4. 80% or more passes. A module is complete when both the video and the quiz are done.
  5. The person is fully compliant once their required modules are done. A completion certificate (PDF) then appears on their dashboard to download.

When someone fails

The quiz closes and says You did not pass, with the score. Retake quiz opens a fresh one with the questions in a new order. After 3 fails in a row on the same module, they are asked to watch the video again first.

What you see

On Training status each person has a progress bar and a tile for every module, grouped by the set of training it belongs to. A tile says where the module stands: Done with the date it is valid until, Not started, started but not finished, or a note such as 2 failed · last 60% for someone who has tried the quiz and not yet passed. Choose Failed a quiz in the filter to list only those people.

Every attempt is recorded with the person, the module, the score and the time.

Automatic reminders and escalation

So nobody has to remember to chase. Turn it on with the switch at the top of Escalation.

  1. The person gets an email 30, 15, 7 and 3 days before a module is due, on the day, and every day after until it is done. Each module is its own email: three modules due is three emails.
  2. A module is due when it runs out. Something never done is due 30 days after the person was added.
  3. Once it is overdue, up to four people are told in turn: the administrator, the administrator's manager, their manager, and top management. By default that is after 1, 7, 15 and 30 days overdue, and you can change each number. A level is told once, then every 7 days until the module is done.
  4. Every email to the chain copies in everyone above and says who else has been told. Leave a level empty to skip it.

Reminders go out once a day, at the hour you choose. What was sent, at the bottom of the screen, lists every email and whether it arrived, for an auditor who asks whether people were chased. It needs email to be set up, on the Email tab in Settings.

Training modules and the internet

Offset Aware runs on your own server and does not need the internet. There is one exception.

The training videos are downloaded when you ask for them. The Windows installer asks which course packs you want, and the Course packs tab in Settings adds one at any time. Each comes from Offset Security's release page and is checked before it is used. That is the only time Offset Aware uses the internet. Nothing about your staff or their training is ever sent anywhere.
  • The Training tab in Settings shows which modules are on this server and who each set applies to. Staff see only those, and only those count towards being fully trained.
  • To add a pack later, tick it on the Course packs tab in Settings and click Download and install. It applies to nobody until you choose who.
  • On a server with no internet, download the pack on another computer and unzip it into the folder shown on the Course packs tab, keeping the file names.
  • Check for updates, on the Updates tab, also asks the internet, but only when you click it.

When training runs out

Awareness training is not done once. Every framework expects it to be repeated, and auditors look for yearly.

  1. A module counts for 12 months from the day it was finished. Change this on the Training tab in Settings, under How long training stays valid: 6, 12, 24 or 36 months, or never.
  2. When it runs out, the person sees it as Run out and must watch the video and pass the quiz again. Until they do, they are not fully trained.
  3. Training status shows the date each module runs out, in amber in its last 30 days. Filter it to Running out in 30 days or Has run out.
  4. Reminders include modules that have run out, so one email chases both.

Every time somebody finishes a module is kept. Download history (CSV) on Training status gives an auditor every completion, with the date and the date it ran out.

Email setup

Email sends sign-in details, password resets and reminders. Aware works without it, but cannot send any of those.

Set it up

  1. Open Settings and choose the Email tab.
  2. Fill in the boxes using the table below.
  3. Click Save.
  4. Click Send test email. A message is sent to your own administrator email address, and how it went appears beneath the buttons.
ProviderHostPortUserPasswordSecure box
Resendsmtp.resend.com465resendyour API keyTick
SendGridsmtp.sendgrid.net587apikeyyour API keyUntick
Mailgunsmtp.mailgun.org587postmaster@yourdomain.comyour SMTP passwordUntick
Microsoft 365smtp.office365.com587the full mailbox addressan app passwordUntick
Google Workspace or Gmailsmtp.gmail.com587the full email addressan app passwordUntick
The secure connection box has to match the port. Tick it for port 465, untick it for port 587. A mismatch fails with a handshake error and no other hint.

Which provider

  • Resend, SendGrid, Mailgun are services built for this, and more reliable than a company mailbox. With Resend, add and verify your domain first. The Send emails as address must be on that domain: otherwise the settings save, the test says it was sent, and nothing arrives.
  • Microsoft 365 and Google will not accept your normal password, because of multi-factor sign-in. Create an app password. Microsoft: mysignins.microsoft.com/security-info, then Add sign-in method, then App password. Google: Account security, 2-Step Verification, App passwords. Microsoft 365 also needs Authenticated SMTP switched on for that mailbox.

Stopping the "Not secure" warning

Aware is always encrypted. Browsers warn only because they do not know the certificate it made for itself.

Fix it once, for everyone, with a certificate from your IT team. It costs nothing: you do not need to buy one.

  1. Ask your IT team for a certificate for the name people type to reach Aware, such as aware.yourcompany.local, issued by your company's own certificate authority. Every company computer already trusts it. Ask for a .pfx file with a password, or for a certificate and key as .pem / .crt and .key files.
  2. Open Settings and choose the HTTPS tab.
  3. Under HTTPS certificate, choose the file, or the certificate and key together. Type the password if there is one.
  4. Click Upload certificate, then reload the page. The padlock appears.

It is checked before it is used. A wrong password, a key that does not belong to the certificate, or an expired certificate is refused, with the reason. A certificate for a different name, a self-signed one, or one about to expire is shown to you first, with Use it anyway and Cancel.

Nothing restarts. The new certificate is in use as soon as it is saved. Old http:// links are sent on to https://.

Every year

Certificates expire. Upload the renewed one the same way. The card shows the expiry date and warns 30 days before.

Still warned after uploading?

  • The name in the address bar is not on the certificate. localhost, the server's IP address and aware.yourcompany.local are different names. The card shows which names it covers.
  • The browser has not been restarted since IT added the company's certificate authority to the computer.
  • It is Firefox, which keeps its own list. IT can turn on security.enterprise_roots.enabled so it uses the Windows one.

Remove uploaded certificate puts Aware's own certificate back. The certificate is kept in the data folder, so a backup of that folder includes its private key: keep backups private.

Updates and backups

Updates

The Updates tab in Settings shows the version installed. Check for updates looks at Offset Security's release page. Nothing is downloaded or installed until you ask.

  • On Docker, Install update does it for you. A second container pulls the new version and restarts Aware, and puts the old version back if the new one does not start.
  • On Windows, it gives you a link. Download the new installer and run it. Your data, port and settings are kept.

Backups

Everything Aware holds is in two places inside its data folder:

NameWhat is in it
database.sqlitePeople, training records and their history, quiz attempts and settings.
uploadsCertificates people have uploaded.

On Windows the data folder is C:\ProgramData\Offset Security\ISO Training Portal. On Docker it is the data volume. The folder kept its old name, so updates find your data. Copy both somewhere safe regularly. For a clean copy, take it when nobody is using Aware.

Uninstalling Aware does not delete this folder.

Locked out of the administrator account

For when the only administrator has forgotten their password.

  • On Windows: open the Start menu, find Offset Security, and click Reset administrator password. Accept the permission prompt, type the administrator's Employee ID, then a new password of at least 8 characters.
  • On Docker: run docker compose exec portal node reset_admin_password.js and answer the same two questions.

It only works for administrator accounts. It cannot show you the old password.
